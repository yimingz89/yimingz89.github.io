About these ratings

HSK 3.0 (2021 vocabulary standard), not the revised 2026 exam list. Levels 1–6 and band 7–9 use exact word entries, not character levels. “Not listed” does not mean advanced.

Frequency: wordfreq’s Chinese usage estimates through about 2021. One extra Zipf point means roughly 10× more frequent. Our labels: 6+ extremely common, 5+ very common, 4+ common, 3+ less common, below 3 rare. No exact record means unknown, not rare.

Frequency combines pronunciations and meanings. A single character’s score counts it as a separate token, not its appearances inside compound words. Sources & methodology

Dictionary definitions, not contextual translations. Names, new slang, and word boundaries may need a closer look.

Keyboard: Tab into the text, use ← / → to move between words, Enter to look up, Esc to close. Saved on this device.

No login. No text uploads. Reading text and translations stay in this browser, not across devices.
